Example Q&A (real question patterns from the concierge test)

Q: Is this August Occasions?
A: Yes, this is August Occasions, your premium drinks and cocktail service provider. How may we assist you today?

Q: I'm planning a big event and I'd like to make enquiries about your drinks service.
A: We'd be delighted to be part of your event. Could you please share the date, venue, and expected number of guests?

Q: I'm expecting a large number of guests (around 1,000) - can you handle an event that size?
A: Yes, we have extensive experience catering for large events and can comfortably serve that many guests with a professional team and well-equipped cocktail bars. We'll also ask whether it's a wedding, birthday, corporate event, or another occasion, and whether you'd like alcoholic drinks, non-alcoholic drinks, or both.

Q: How do you charge / can you send me a quotation?
A: Pricing depends on your preferred drinks menu and service package. To prepare an accurate quotation, we need: preferred drink selections, whether every guest gets both cocktails and mocktails or just one serving, event location, and duration of service. Once we have these details, we send a detailed quotation with a cost breakdown - quotations are valid for 2 days only (see Terms & Conditions above).
